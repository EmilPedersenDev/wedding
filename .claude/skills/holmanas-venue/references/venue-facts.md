# Holmanäs Gård — verified fact sheet

Sources: the venue's planning sheet *"Att tänka på inför festen på Holmanäs"* (from Emma at
Holmanäs), the venue's *Ordningsregler* (house rules), and <https://www.holmanas.se/Om-oss> /
`/Kontakt`. Scope is deliberately limited to facts that can shape **guest-facing** copy — rental
pricing, cleaning obligations, damage charges and catering-supplier recommendations are the
couple's internal business and are intentionally not recorded here.

Last checked: 2026-08-17.

## Identity and location

- Name: **Holmanäs Gård** (Holmanäs Gård AB).
- Address: **Lyckebovägen 248, 231 93 Trelleborg**.
- Setting: Skåne, near **Höllviken**, close to **Falsterbonäset** and **Söderslätt**.
- **~20 minutes by car from Malmö.**
- Landscape: grain fields running right up to the buildings, the sea on the horizon.
- Venue contact: Emma — `info@holmanas.se` / `emma@holmanas.se`, 0708-842152.
  *This is the venue's contact, not a guest contact. Do not publish it on the site.*

## The buildings

- The farm was **built in the mid-1800s** and carefully renovated in recent years.
- The party space is a converted **barn/loge**: whitewashed stone walls, exposed concrete floors,
  original stable windows alongside large modern glass sections for daylight.
- Spaces available: entrance hall with coat area, the main hall (flexible layouts), a separate
  **bar and lounge** area, a **warming kitchen** (catering-grade, but food is cooked off site and
  only reheated here), toilets, and an adjoining **mingle terrace** looking out over the fields.
- An **innergård** (inner courtyard) is used for drop-off and as mingle space.
- The venue is rented for **three days**.

## Capacity

- **Fire-safety maximum: 130 people in the venue at one time.** Hard cap, from the house rules.
- **100 chairs** (black, HAY) — the practical seated-dinner ceiling is 100, not 130.
- Round tables: 180 cm diameter, seat **8–10** guests each.
- Rectangular tables: 180 × 75 cm, seat **3–3.5 per side**.

## Accommodation on the farm

- **Up to 13 double rooms across 4 houses** (venue planning sheet).
- holmanas.se states **15 double rooms** (noted as available from 2020).
- ⚠️ **Conflict — 13 vs 15 double rooms is unresolved.** `wedding.ts` currently claims
  "26 sovplatser", which matches 13 rooms. Confirm with Emma before publishing any figure.
- Houses are let whole (a 3-room house and a 4-room house are the two configurations mentioned).
- The venue advises booking rooms **well in advance**.
- Staying over the night *before* the wedding, e.g. with bridesmaids/best men, is common here.

## Access times (rental, not guest-facing)

- Check-in **Thursday 18.00**, check-out **Sunday 14.00**, unless otherwise agreed.
- (For Wednesday parties: check-in Monday 18.00, check-out Thursday 14.00.)
- Relevant to guest copy only insofar as everything, including guests' own decorations and
  belongings, must be off the property by Sunday 14.00.

## Arrival, parking, transport

- Unloading and drop-off happen **on the innergård**; cars then move to the **large car park
  outside Holmanäs**.
- **Taxis collect guests at the large car park**, not at the courtyard.
- No public-transport detail is given by the venue. The nearest town referenced is Höllviken.
  ⚠️ The site's placeholder bus route (Malmö C → Höllviken → 10 min taxi) is **unverified**.

## House rules that affect what guests are told

- **The doors toward the field must be kept closed from 22.00** out of consideration for the
  neighbours. Music effectively moves indoors at 22.00.
- **Smoking is not permitted indoors** — outside only.
- **Fireworks and rockets are forbidden** on the property.
- Indoor furniture stays indoors.
- Decorations may be fixed to ceiling and beams with drawing pins or fine nails. **Nothing may be
  attached to the stone walls, and no tape on floors, walls or ceilings.**
- Confetti is allowed but must be picked up — worth knowing before promising guests confetti.

## Included in the rental (shapes what guests need to bring or expect)

- Glassware, porcelain and cutlery are included: champagne, white wine, red wine, water, grappa,
  cognac, schnapps and drink glasses; plates in three sizes suited to a three-course dinner;
  starter, soup, main, dessert and coffee cutlery.
- Also included: 2 champagne coolers, water carafes, small vases, ~50 brass candlesticks, coffee
  cups and pots, a coffee maker with filters, and **candles** (tapers, tea lights, block candles in
  all the lanterns indoors and out).
- **Sound system** with sub and mixer is included. If used outdoors it must be brought in before
  the party ends.
- ⚠️ **No AV equipment** — no screen, no projector. Any slideshow or speech visuals must be rented
  in via a DJ or party-rental firm. Do not let site copy imply a slideshow is possible.
- **Holmanäs is a "do it yourself" venue with no staff on site.** The couple and their helpers run
  the day. Guests should not expect venue staff to ask.
